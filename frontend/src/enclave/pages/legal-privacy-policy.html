Privacy policy

Last updated 25 September 2026

Terms of servicePrivacy policyRisk disclosure

01. Scope

This notice describes the Enclave development pilot, its hosted backend and wallet connection. E1 production has not been released.

02. Prompt and preview data

Public paid inference is not enabled. Authorized operator requests use the backend: the browser encrypts the prompt for the gateway, and the configured NEAR adapter requires hardware verification before sending a prompt to the provider. The gateway currently runs in software on a conventional VPS. Do not treat it as a gateway with hardware-protected keys.

03. Storage

Workspace, receipt, payment and wallet-account records are stored on the backend and survive reloads. Wallet sign-in stores a hash of the session token on the server and uses a Secure, HttpOnly cookie that expires 30 minutes after signing. A public wallet-selection hint is stored in your browser. WalletConnect also stores its connection session. Signing out revokes the login; disconnecting removes the selection hint and ends the active wallet connection.

04. Hosting and technical requests

Loading the website and calling its API sends ordinary network requests to the hosting server. Hosting and network providers may process IP addresses and technical logs to deliver and secure the service. WalletConnect connections use its relay and wallet directory; connecting shares the approved wallet address and network.

05. Analytics, media and social links

The X and Telegram links open the external services in a new tab. Following a link is subject to that service’s privacy terms; it does not send a message or subscribe you automatically.

06. Exports and selective disclosure

Audit exports include loaded receipt and payment metadata for the selected scope. They exclude prompt and response plaintext. Downloaded files remain on your device unless you share them.

07. Your choices

Sign out to revoke the current login, or disconnect the wallet to end its connection. Reloading or closing a tab does not erase backend records or revoke an unexpired login. You can remove browser storage and downloaded files yourself; this does not delete server records. Avoid sensitive production data during the pilot.

08. Production privacy

Before production launch, the operator must publish its identity, retention periods and privacy contact, and review this notice against the final deployment. This pilot notice does not establish those missing operational details.
