Privacy policy

Terms of servicePrivacy policyRisk disclosure

01. Scope

This notice describes this Enclave website and its browser-based product preview. It does not claim that the planned production inference service is already operating.

02. Prompt and preview data

The dashboard processes entered prompts locally in your browser. It uses preset illustrative responses and local hashing. The website does not send your prompt to a model endpoint. Preview receipts contain hashes and metadata, rather than the entered prompt or response text.

03. Storage

Preview agents, receipts and usage records are held in page memory and reset on reload. A session flag may be stored in this tab to avoid repeating the entry animation. Exported JSON and CSV files are saved to your device only when you request a download.

04. Hosting and technical requests

Loading the website requires ordinary requests to its hosting provider. The provider may process technical information such as IP address, browser information and request logs to deliver and secure the website. Access to a private preview may be managed by the hosting platform under its own privacy terms.

05. Analytics, media and social links

This website does not add advertising pixels, behavioural analytics or third-party social embeds. Website fonts and selected images are served as local site assets. The X and Telegram controls open a coming-soon dialog and do not transmit messages or subscribe you to a service.

06. Exports and selective disclosure

Audit exports include only the preview records and optional illustrative payment amounts you select. They exclude prompt and response plaintext. After download, you decide whether to retain or share those files.

07. Your choices

You can reset the preview with its reset control, reload the page to discard in-memory records, close the tab to end the session, or delete downloaded files from your device. Do not enter sensitive production information into the demonstration.

08. Production privacy

A production Enclave service will require a privacy notice identifying its operator, actual processing purposes, infrastructure providers, retention practices and applicable rights channels. Those operational details are not established by the concept PDFs or this preview.
